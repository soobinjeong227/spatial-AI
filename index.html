1. One structure per lot. Streets can't hold structures.
  2. Zoning: houses go on residential lots, shops on commercial lots, parks on green lots.
  3. Street access: a structure's lot must touch a street.
  4. You can't remove a street if a neighbor would lose its only access.
  5. Only an empty lot can be rezoned.
  6. Houses and shops have 1–3 floors.
  7. A house holds 2 residents per floor.
  8. When a house is demolished, its residents move to the nearest house with room, or leave.
  9. A shop offers 3 jobs per floor.
  10. Residents only work at shops the streets connect to their home.
  11. A park holds at most 4 trees.
